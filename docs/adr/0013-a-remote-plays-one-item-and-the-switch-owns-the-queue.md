# A remote plays one item; the switch owns the queue

**Status:** accepted

On Android, playback can move from the phone to a **Remote Output**: a Cast receiver or a Sonos room. `RemoteSwitchingPlayer` (the switch) stands in front of the phone's ExoPlayer and every remote output's player.

While a remote output plays, the switch keeps the **Queue** itself, in `RemoteQueue`. It resolves the current **Track**'s URL and sends the remote output that one item. When that item ends, the switch sends the next. The remote output never holds a queue.

This applies to Cast too, though a Cast receiver can hold a queue of its own.

## Why

- **Sonos holds one URI.** A Sonos room plays the one URI it was given (`SetAVTransportURI`); its own queue is for its music library, not for arbitrary stream URLs. One model for both remote outputs therefore has to be one item.
- **A URL is resolved just before it plays.** A **Transform** can return a short-lived URL (a signed or tokenised one). Resolving each item when it is sent keeps that URL fresh; a queue sent ahead would hold URLs that expire before their turn.
- **Cast's receiver window and its reconciliation went.** A Cast queue meant keeping a window of items on the receiver and reconciling it with the phone's queue after every edit, skip and end. With one item there is nothing to reconcile.

## Considered options

- **Each remote output keeps its own queue** — rejected: Sonos can't, and Cast's queue had to be reconciled with the phone's queue on every change.
- **Cast keeps a receiver queue, Sonos plays one item** — rejected: two queue models behind one switch, each with its own bugs.
- **The switch owns the queue and sends one item (chosen)** — one queue model, held where it is tested.

## Consequences

- **A Cast track queue has a short gap between tracks.** The receiver can't preload the next item, so it is briefly idle between items while the next one loads.
- **Skips from outside the phone do nothing on Cast.** The receiver holds no next or previous item, so a skip from a TV remote, a voice command or another device's Cast notification has nothing to move to. Skips from the phone, its notification and Android Auto work.
- **A receiver queue for Cast is #211.** It is to come as a capability of the Cast remote output, which the switch keeps the queue for. It will not be a second queue model.
- **A remote output's player is one-item.** A new remote output implements `RemotePlayback`: it plays the item it is given and reports when that item plays, ends or fails.
